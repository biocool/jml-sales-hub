01 / Open

The opener

Introduce

Use this opener only if you actually checked this restaurant on both Google and ChatGPT. Otherwise, choose the Google-only opener.

Hi, I’m Elia with JML Restaurant Growth. Our visibility team checked how your restaurant shows up on Google and in ChatGPT. We spotted a few things on your Google Business Profile that could make it harder for people to find you. Can I show you one quick example?

Say “a few things” only if you verified multiple findings. For one finding, say “We spotted one thing on your Google Business Profile that could make it harder for people to find you.” For none, use the no-verified-finding path.

If they only declined the offer, not the conversation

Use these only if they are still willingly talking. A clear no, silence, or a busy room means thank them and go.

Ask permission: “No problem. Would you mind if I asked one quick question before I go?” If they agree, choose one question:

  • Reason: “What made this not a fit for you today?”
  • Who handles it: “Do they also handle your Google listing day to day, or does someone here take care of it?”
  • Unmet need: “Is there anything about your Google listing you wish were easier to manage?”

Decision flow

Your path

    Before you walk in

    Confirm the exact location and retest one guest-facing detail. Keep the one-page printout handy. If it’s a rush, come back rather than stopping staff mid-service.

    After you walk out

    Log the outcome, the actual wording used, and where the conversation changed. Note staff/manager, the finding, and any follow-up your contact agreed to. Don’t record contact details in this file.

    Training only · Two-week pilot

    See where the conversation stops

    After each visit, mark these three checkpoints in the existing campaign tracker. Use Yes, No, or Not reached.

    1. Decision-maker reached: Did you speak to the person who handles or decides on the Google listing?
    2. Opener heard: Did that person hear the full opener and get a chance to respond?
    3. Next step agreed: Was a specific next step mutually agreed? A handout by itself isn’t a follow-up.

    Optional: note the exact words and when the conversation shifted, without guessing why. After two weeks, compare which opener reached each checkpoint in similar situations. Don’t record prospect notes on this page.
