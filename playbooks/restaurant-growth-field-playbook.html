01 / Open

The opener

Introduce

Introduce yourself, identify the program, and ask permission. Stop and let them answer.

Hi, I’m Elia with JML Restaurant Growth. We help restaurants make it easier for guests to find their menu and book through Google. I noticed something on your Google listing. Can I show you?

No finding you can reproduce? Use the no-verified-finding path instead.

Decision flow

Your path

    Before you walk in

    Confirm the exact location and retest one guest-facing detail. Keep the one-page printout handy. If it’s a rush, come back rather than stopping staff mid-service.

    After you walk out

    Log the outcome, the actual wording used, and where the conversation changed. Note staff/manager, the finding, and any follow-up your contact agreed to. Don’t record contact details in this file.
