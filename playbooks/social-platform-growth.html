Research window · Nov 21, 2024 to Nov 21, 2026

Use evidence without guessing the algorithm.

Official disclosures describe these surfaces, features or policy changes. Research is labeled by method and limitations; current undated Help pages clarify mechanics but are not dated releases. JML tactics are hypotheses to test, not implied platform rules.

Test contract: set a goal, organic baseline, comparable audience/cohort and one primary metric. Change one variable; keep creator, topic, format, length and schedule as comparable as practical. Capture an early check and seven-day results; flag major external events. Compare medians, show counts and sample size, and extend inconclusive tests instead of declaring winners. Separate organic/paid and distinguish follows from qualified outcomes.

Use rates honestly: dividing follows by reach does not prove attribution when an account-wide count is used. Report the time window, population, numerator and denominator; mark missing fields “not available.” Don’t compare views across platforms as if definitions matched.

LinkedIn

Become known for a useful professional point of view.

Discovery → professional Feed, relevant replies and follows recommendations. Conversion → member profile or brand Page. Retention → repeat conversations, series or newsletters where available.

Official disclosures

  • 2026-03-12 · LinkedIn Engineering: Feed ranking
    LinkedIn describes retrieval and sequential ranking using professional profile information and a history of engagement/ignoring content, balancing freshness and relevance. Distribution includes suggested posts beyond a member’s network.
  • 2026-03-12 · LinkedIn: authentic professional content
    LinkedIn says it is reducing recycled content and engagement bait and acting against comment automation and pods.
  • 2026-08-24 · LinkedIn Engineering: follows recommendations
    LinkedIn rebuilt creator discovery on My Network and Home Feed around creator/member relevance. Follows recommendations and post ranking are separate systems.

Research and other evidence

2025 · Original observational study: What Predicts Engagement on LinkedIn?
A study of 991 sampled posts relates message features such as tags to recorded engagement. It cannot isolate the effect of a feature on a particular account or establish feed weights; do not tag people who have no substantive connection to the post.

Not established: No published universal format ranking, external-link penalty, required early comment window, or transferable Page-versus-member multiplier.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Choose one role and recurring decision: e.g., an operations lead deciding whether to automate a handoff. Put that promise in the member headline/About, or in the Page description and featured content. Create a weekly proof-led series rather than generic AI commentary.

Formats & production

Use a first-hand text or image post for one argued point; a document for a process people can use; a native video when a demo is the evidence. Treat these as format choices, not rank shortcuts. A member shares personal judgment; the Page shares approved company evidence, not a ghostwritten first-person story.

Community & organic interaction

Join a small list of relevant threads with comments that add evidence or a better question. Reply substantively to your own commenters and log recurring questions. Partner only with people who actually contributed; no pods, canned DM outreach, bulk invitations or comment automation.

Creator workflow

SME claim → one proof/link with consent → select format → owner approves voice → publish → community owner responds → analyst logs what qualified audience members asked. Repurpose the strongest question into a follow-up.

What to measure

Qualified comments/replies; impressions and members reached when available; follows per 1,000 impressions only when same-entity attribution exists (otherwise weekly net follows); profile/Page visits to qualified inquiries; response time and analyst-tagged lead quality. Keep member and Page scorecards separate.

Four-week experiment

  1. Baseline four comparable posts; capture role fit from public replies; define what qualifies.
  2. Run matched posts on one topic: lived example vs general recommendation; keep creator and format constant.
  3. Repeat with a second topic; respond with added substance and log follow-up questions.
  4. Compare median qualified replies and same-entity follows at seven days; repeat winning angle, not a single viral post.

Instagram

Earn discovery with original work, then give people a reason to return.

Discovery → Reels and other recommended surfaces. Conversion → profile, pinned posts, follow or inbound DM. Retention → series, Stories, and replies. Do not infer a single ranking system across surfaces.

Official disclosures

  • 2024-12-10 / 2025-06-26 update · Meta: Trial Reels
    Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first; some followers may still encounter one via shares or elsewhere. Meta describes initial metrics after roughly 24 hours and optional automatic sharing based on views in the first 72 hours. Check whether Trial is present in the account.
  • 2026-01-28 · Meta: Q4 originality disclosure
    Meta reported a greater share of original posts in U.S. recommendations in Q4 2025. This is an aggregate company disclosure, not a guaranteed lift or ranking coefficient.

Research and other evidence

2026 · Primary industry study: Buffer’s 2026 engagement dataset
Observational data from publishing-tool users flag differences by channel and format; not a random sample, and a format result does not isolate ranking, account quality or audience. Use the same cohort caveat for Facebook and X.

Not established: No verified universal weight for DM sends, likes, audio or hashtags; no guaranteed reach threshold or boost from the Trial switch. Account Status and actual metrics determine what you can use.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Promise a visual payoff: e.g., restaurant owners learn one filming idea each Tuesday. Design Reels for a stranger and pin a ‘start here’ post, strongest proof and clear next action; label partnerships and get likeness/asset permission.

Formats & production

Hook with actual proof in the opening seconds; caption speech; keep examples legible on phone. Use one clear carousel outcome per card; use Stories for FAQs and polls, and Collab only with a genuine contributor. Treat these as separate jobs.

Community & organic interaction

Answer real DMs and comments during assigned coverage; privately route order or customer-service issues without exposing personal data. Turn repeated questions into educational follow-ups. Invite collaborations with genuine audience overlap rather than engagement exchanges.

Creator workflow

Capture from an approved shot list → edit and subtitle → validate cover and rights → check eligibility/Trial availability → publish with accessibility text → monitor → save first-party reports and qualitative themes.

What to measure

Unique accounts reached; percentage non-follower reach; average watch time where available; shares/saves per 1,000 unique reach; profile visits and attributable follows separately when unavailable; qualified inbound DMs with permission; unscheduled team time.

Four-week experiment

  1. Record organic reach, visits and follows separately for Reels/Stories/carousels.
  2. Test two opening treatments on otherwise comparable new Reels; use Trial where offered and record settings.
  3. Repeat; preserve topic, duration range and weekday window; do not mix Trial with normal-Reel cohorts.
  4. Use 24-hour and seven-day snapshots; promote the variant with repeatable audience fit, not views alone.

Facebook

Build repeat trust around a place, topic or recurring community problem.

Discovery → Page Feed and Reels; discussion → Groups subject to rules; conversion → Page, Event, relevant inbox or owned site. Keep Page and Group measurement separate.

Official disclosures

  • 2026-03-13 · Meta: Rewarding Original Creators on Facebook
    Meta says it prioritizes original content in Feed/Reels, deprioritizes copies and low-value edits, and may deem primarily unoriginal accounts non-recommendable. Meaningfully adding information or analysis differs from simple reaction or reupload.
  • 2026-01-28 · Meta: Q4 Facebook ranking update
    Meta reported aggregate organic feed/video improvements and more same-day Reels; no claim a schedule or duration guarantees reach.

Research and other evidence

2026-04 · Primary field experiment: Social Media Comments (working paper)
Randomized visibility and stance of comments in a Facebook social-issue campaign. A visible comment section increased measured engagement in that study. The campaign, topic and experimental delivery are not evidence that organic Page or Group comments are assigned a ranking bonus.

Not established: No verified Group-to-Page distribution rule, precise comment weighting, group repost multiplier or universal preference for links, native media or posting frequency.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Pick one legitimate community usefulness promise: e.g., weekly local event logistics and accessible booking information. Set Page category/about/location; pin an updated resource. A Group is appropriate only with a moderation owner, a community purpose and documented entry rules.

Formats & production

Film original local demos or FAQs; publish Page posts/events with accurate details; adapt the actual question for a Group only where moderators permit it. No copy-pasted multi-group promotion or minor-edited downloads from other creators.

Community & organic interaction

Assign member questions, spam and safety moderation; honor Group rules, keep sensitive customer cases in approved private channels; close the loop on recurring public questions with corrected posts or FAQs. Obtain permission before featuring a community member.

Creator workflow

Pull a community question → source and film original answer → Page approval → optional moderator-approved discussion → community owner handles replies → measure Page vs Group separately, keep proof of rights.

What to measure

Organic Page reach, content interactions with raw counts, follows where available, qualified messages/event RSVPs, median first-response time and repeat Group contributors. Exclude paid reach and treat RSVPs as intent, not attendance.

Four-week experiment

  1. Baseline organic Page outcomes and Group discussion separately; record admin rules.
  2. Publish original FAQ Reel and local utility post; compare matching topic/format cohorts, not raw totals.
  3. Repeat the stronger question, add moderator-approved discussion only if appropriate.
  4. Review follower fit, substantive threads and qualified messages; continue only if useful responses outweigh moderation load.

YouTube

Turn a viewer’s real question into a channel they return to.

Discovery → Home/Browse, Suggested, Search or Shorts feed; conversion → subscribe or next video; retention → repeat viewers, playlists, Community posts. Distinguish surfaces, formats and cohorts.

Official disclosures

  • 2026-09-01 · Creator Insider: Interview with YouTube Growth and Discovery
    YouTube’s discovery lead discusses ranking recommendations for individual viewers rather than applying a single aggregate metric as a threshold.
  • 2026-05 · TeamYouTube: search, discovery and AI labels
    YouTube describes recommendations as responding to observable viewer behavior and long-term satisfaction, not the AI disclosure label alone.
  • 2026-08-19 · YouTube: starts and engaged views
    A views-definition change for August 24 separates starts from engaged viewing; YouTube says it is not a recommendation change. Confirm metrics in Studio and avoid unadjusted time-series comparisons across definitions.
  • Current Help · checked 2026-11-21 · YouTube: Shorts search and discovery
    YouTube lists view/ignore choices, watch duration and share viewed, plus satisfaction and other factors; Search also considers query fit and viewing behavior. No minimum posting cadence is required.

Research and other evidence

2026-07-07 · Official practice examples, not independent evidence
YouTube showcases comments, polls and reply Shorts as community tools. These are sourced creator practices, not independent ranking evidence. No clean independent causal result for the recommended workflow was verified.

Not established: No universal retention threshold, thumbnail winner guaranteed beyond its test, or publicly applicable ranking equation; timing, traffic source, video length and format change comparisons.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Map three exact questions from buyers or viewers. Make an answer library and a recurring follow-up series. Channel promise should describe who returns and why; feature the most useful start here video and organize playlists by problem.

Formats & production

Write a clear title and cover promise; answer it early, then show the proof and next step. Use Shorts for one useful takeaway; use long-form when a walkthrough needs it. Offer a relevant sequence, not a generic subscription prompt.

Community & organic interaction

Pin a useful clarifying question, listen to comments, answer with a follow-up or Short when warranted. Moderate risky or harmful suggestions; distinguish customer support from technical public advice.

Creator workflow

Research search terms and own comments → validate script claim/rights → record clear hook, proof and solution → add chapters/captions → publish → review CTR plus retention by source, send to next related video.

What to measure

Traffic-source cohorts; impressions CTR for eligible surfaces; engaged views and retention; subscribers per 1,000 consistent engaged views where available; returning viewers, next-video click and useful comments. Keep Shorts separate from long-form.

Four-week experiment

  1. Baseline channel metrics by surface and format; gather two common audience questions.
  2. Publish one tightly framed answer and a separate Short; compare its promises with prior videos of similar length.
  3. Repeat another question; use Studio thumbnail experimentation where eligible, not sequential uncontrolled swaps as proof.
  4. Read seven-day and, once mature, 28-day metrics; choose the topic with stronger qualified viewing and follow-through rather than raw starts.

TikTok

Speak the audience’s language and become the useful answer to a narrow question.

Discovery → For You and Search, each with different factors. Conversion → profile, follow, owned resource where available. Retention → recognizable creator series and useful comment follow-ups.

Official disclosures

  • 2025-06-04 · TikTok: ways to shape your feed
    TikTok describes For You topic preferences and eligibility at a platform level. A setting or content being eligible does not establish rank or guarantee reach.
  • Current Help · checked 2026-11-21 · TikTok: how content is recommended
    TikTok groups signals into interactions, content and user information. For You generally weights interactions including time more heavily; Search typically weights content/query fit more heavily. No universal weights are disclosed.
  • 2025-08-14 · TikTok: stronger Community Guidelines
    Dated announcement of updated guidelines effective September 13. Recommendation eligibility and safety policy are separate from scoring.

Research and other evidence

2026-01-14 · TikTok Next 2026: platform marketing forecast
Useful way to source audience language and hypotheses, not independent evidence, causal research or algorithm disclosure. A current independent platform-specific causal source for these tactics was not verified.

Not established: No trustworthy universal initial test-pool size, mandatory trending sound, retention threshold or hashtag formula. Moderation or ineligibility can cut exposure independent of viewer preference.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Interview a customer-facing person and collect exact questions from search suggestions and public comments. Pick one recurring need, such as “what is actually included in a session?” Put niche and series promise clearly in the profile and pinned content.

Formats & production

One question, one filmed answer, one next step. Say and display the exact question when natural. Use a second video for the deeper answer; reuse assets only with rights and edit natively, not as an unaltered repost.

Community & organic interaction

Respond to good-faith comments and turn real questions into videos with permission where needed. Use available Creator Care Mode or moderation controls; escalate harassment or private client details. Keep a repeatable creator/SME slot.

Creator workflow

Question bank → one-take script → film proof and subtitle → rights and eligibility check → publish → reply using real details → tag traffic source and comments in reporting.

What to measure

For You vs Search traffic; average watch time and completion for comparable lengths; profile visits and attributable follows where shown; qualified inbound inquiries and substantive comments. Track production effort per post.

Four-week experiment

  1. Pick a question cluster and establish For You/Search baselines on comparable clips.
  2. Test problem-first versus demonstration-first opening on closely matched new clips.
  3. Repeat on another question; publish one relevant answer to a public comment.
  4. Compare seven-day medians by traffic source and one qualified-intent metric. Treat small samples as directional.

X

Earn followership through a consistent point of view and useful public conversations.

Discovery → For You and niche replies; Following, Search and Communities differ. Conversion → credible profile, pinned explainer, follow. Retention → repeat conversations and timely original analysis.

Official disclosures

  • 2026-08 to 2026-10 · X/xAI: public For You recommendation repository
    Public For You implementation and changelog describe candidate retrieval, predicted activities, scoring and visibility filtering; later notes address read-through/time-spent predictions. Prediction weights are not a raw likes-to-reach formula or a guarantee that any production configuration is fixed.
  • 2025-04 · X: Authenticity policy
    Policy prohibits spam and coordinated inauthentic activity, including repetitive unsolicited or irrelevant replies. Do not buy engagement, use copied reply batches or join exchanges.

Research and other evidence

2026 · Primary industry study: Buffer’s observational data
Useful context from Buffer users; selection, platform/API metrics and plan differences limit inference. Not evidence that a subscription or action causes a fixed organic increase.

Not established: Public code is not a promise about all surfaces or weights. No validated universal external-link penalty, ideal reply count, premium multiplier or timing recipe.

JML tactics to test

Audience & follows

Build a small roster of real niche practitioners, conference hosts, and informed customers. Put the niche and profile promise up front, and pin one clear explainer or example. Follow relevant people for research, not follow-back.

Formats & production

Publish a source-backed take, visual example or short thread when the topic needs context; add a perspective in quote posts rather than repeating the original. Time-sensitive content requires verified facts; correct material changes transparently.

Community & organic interaction

Answer questions in a handful of relevant threads with original insight. Reply to inbound replies; route support to an approved private channel. Document moderation and do not mass tag, DM, or recycle comments.

Creator workflow

Watch niche questions → verify sources → write one claim and evidence → approve potentially sensitive posts → publish → reserve time for relevant replies → capture recurring themes and outcomes.

What to measure

Impressions if available, qualified public replies, profile visits and follows, attributable site visits using UTM links, repeat conversations and workload. Mark missing analytics rather than using engagement totals as replacement.

Four-week experiment

  1. Baseline similar recent originals and replies separately; define a qualifying reply or visit.
  2. Test evidence-first versus insight-first post on comparable topics, holding format stable.
  3. Repeat and contribute selectively to the same topic’s relevant discussions; track workload separately.
  4. Compare medians and repeat engagers; keep the approach only if audience fit improves without spam complaints.

Approval, ownership and continuity

Onboard and offboard the right identity.

Brand account · onboarding

Client confirms Page, handle, owner and backup ownership. Grant named individual permissions using native roles; enable MFA on owner accounts. Confirm publishing rights, tone, rights/consent, response SLA, escalation route and analytics baseline. Do not share passwords or create unauthorized accounts.

Member / creator · onboarding

The individual owns their personal profile, audience and voice. Approve how research, drafts and analytics are shared. The member performs actions on personal profiles unless a supported, explicitly authorized workflow exists. Use a content handoff and track approval; never impersonate or borrow credentials.

Brand account · offboarding

Client owner verifies admin access and exports scheduled posts, rights, analytics and moderation records to the approved archive. Transfer operating documentation, pause unapproved schedules and revoke only agency or departing staff roles/tokens after confirming the successor can operate.

Member / creator · offboarding

Return originals and approvals; retain or delete internal copies according to the agreed retention terms. Remove agency access to shared drafts/analytics, not the member’s personal account. Do not transfer or modify their profile or audience; follow the contract for company-owned creative or scheduled company work.

Use the management playbook for approvals, role assignments and escalation. Do not publish uncertain claims, personal data or crisis responses without the designated decision owner.
