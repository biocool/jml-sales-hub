                              0. How to use this system

                              The system has seven parts. Read the Master first. For social content, read CC, PF and the route flow you are working in (RT); then only the Playbook that matches your project.

                              CodeTitleCovers
                              MGMaster GovernanceShared rules, roles, approvals, files, AI
                              CCContent Calendar & Brief StandardWhat the calendar must contain, when a row is ready, capture and file-delivery standards
                              PFProduction Flow, AI & Human in the LoopFlowchart, content types, routes, tools, AI failure points, revisions, automation, handoff
                              RTRoute Production FlowsA flowchart, steps, tools and standards for each route (S, A, B, G) and the Prompt Library
                              PB-01Social & Graphic ContentPosts, carousels, stories, reel covers, infographics, digital ads
                              PB-02Video, Photo & MotionReels, clips, photography, editing, motion, sound and color

                              Boundary rule. Designing a reel cover belongs to PB-01; producing the reel video belongs to PB-02. A print piece adapted for digital (for example a poster resized for Instagram) is designed in PB-03 and published under PB-01.

                              Specs are working defaults. Platform dimensions, safe zones, file sizes and print specs change often. Verify them against the platform's or printer's current requirements before every project.

                              1. Master Governance

                              1.1 Core principles

                              1. No brief, no start. No work begins without an approved brief.
                              2. The client's brand guidelines are the final reference and outrank personal taste.
                              3. One final approver. Every project has exactly one; feedback from several people is consolidated through that person.
                              4. Written, consolidated feedback. Verbal or scattered feedback (separate chats, calls) is not valid until logged in the project's official channel.
                              5. Licensed sources only. Photos, fonts, music, video and templates need a clear license.
                              6. Nothing is published without final approval.
                              7. Keep editable files and final exports separate.
                              8. AI use is transparent and documented (section 1.6).

                              1.2 Roles

                              One person may hold several roles, but the owner of each step must be clear.

                              RoleResponsibility
                              Account / Project ManagerReceives the brief, schedules, communicates with the client
                              Creative LeadCreative direction, internal visual review
                              DesignerGraphic and UI design
                              Photographer / VideographerShooting and sound recording
                              Editor / Motion DesignerEditing, motion, color, audio
                              AI Content OperatorProduces and documents AI-assisted content
                              QC ReviewerRuns the checklist (preferably not the producer)
                              Client ContactSupplies content and feedback
                              Client Final ApproverFinal approval or rejection
                              Content / Social TeamWrites the Content Calendar (CC-01), owns copy, receives the handoff and publishes
                              Automation OwnerBuilds and maintains the automations in 3.8 (may be the Project Manager)

                              RACI. R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed. Each step has exactly one A.

                              Intake → Brief → Planning → Creative → Production → QC → Approval → Delivery → Archive → Review

                              For content that comes through a Content Calendar, the detailed working flow is section 3 (PF), with one flow per route in section 4 (RT), and the calendar row that passes G0 serves as FR-01.

                              1.4 Review and revision rules

                              • Suggested default: two revision rounds per contract (adjust per client contract). Work beyond that is a new request.
                              • Changing an approved brief is a Change Request, with its impact on time and cost recorded.
                              • Each feedback round is sent all at once, not piecemeal.
                              • The client's response deadline per round is stated in the brief; client delays move the delivery date proportionally.
                              • Rush projects are exceptions: record the reason and get the Project Manager's approval before starting.

                              CLIENT_PROJECT/
├── 01_Brief/
├── 02_References/
├── 03_Assets/          (logos, fonts, images, licensed footage)
├── 04_Working_Files/   (PSD, AI, INDD, Figma, PRPROJ, AEP)
├── 05_Review/          (versions sent for review)

                              Naming: Client_Project_ContentType_V01_YYYYMMDD, and Client_Project_ContentType_FINAL for the approved version. Example: Acme_Spring_Instagram_Carousel_V02_20261115

                              • Never overwrite versions; save a new one.
                              • Back up raw and working files: at least two copies in two locations.
                              • Archive retention period and access level are set in the client contract.

                              1.6 AI standard (AI-STD-01)

                              AI is an assistive tool. It is not a source of truth and does not replace professional responsibility.

                              Permitted: ideation, copy drafts, image generation and editing, audio enhancement, motion assistance, translation, preliminary research, automation of repetitive tasks.

                              Prohibited or requires written permission:

                              • Entering a client's confidential information into public AI tools
                              • Publishing AI output without human review
                              • Fake images or video of real people presented as real
                              • Using a person's face, voice or style without consent
                              • Inventing statistics, quotes or documents
                              • Using output with unclear legal or IP status

                              Required record (FR-03): tool and model, date, type of use, input data, degree of human involvement, approval status, and the client's awareness of AI use in the deliverable.

                              1.7 Universal QC checklist

                              • Matches the approved brief
                              • All text and spelling checked (every language used)
                              • Logo, colors and fonts match the brand guidelines
                              • Dimensions and format match the platform or use
                              • All sources are licensed
                              • AI content is recorded
                              • Written final approval received

                              1.8 Decision gates and escalation

                              A gate is a point where work stops until a named person decides. Nothing moves to the next stage while a gate is open.

                              GatePasses whenIf not metDecides
                              G0 Calendar readyThe calendar row meets the Definition of Ready (2.3)Row returned to the content team with a missing-field list. No production startsAssigned producer
                              G1 BriefFR-01 is complete and the client approved the brief in writingStop. No planning or production startsProject Manager
                              G2 CreativeConcept, moodboard or script is approvedBack to Creative. No production on an unapproved conceptCreative Lead
                              G3 QCUniversal and playbook checklists are fully ticked by someone other than the producerBack to Production. Counts as an internal fix, not a client revision roundQC Reviewer
                              G4 Client approvalWritten approval received (FR-02)Revision round per 1.4; beyond the cap it becomes a Change RequestClient Final Approver
                              G5 CloseFinal exports delivered, folder complete, version recordedThe project stays open until archivedProject Manager

                              Escalate to the Project Manager, then to the Creative & Production Lead, when: the delivery date is at risk · the revision cap is reached · the client asks to change an approved brief · a license, release or AI-output status is in doubt · the client misses the response deadline · the same item fails QC twice.

                              Pause or exit (suggested default): if client input is still missing after the response deadline, pause the project and move the delivery date proportionally (1.4). Closing a project early requires a recorded reason and the Creative & Production Lead's sign-off.

                              1.9 Never-do list (applies to every playbook)

                              1. Start production without an approved brief (G1).
                              2. Change an approved brief without a Change Request.
                              3. Overwrite a version; always save a new one.
                              4. Use any image, font, music or footage without a license.
                              5. Publish or deliver anything without written client approval.
                              6. Enter a client's confidential information into a public AI tool.
                              7. Publish AI output without human review, or invent statistics, quotes or documents.
                              8. Use a person's face, voice or image without a signed release.
                              9. Let the producer be the only QC reviewer of their own work, where team size allows.
                              10. Treat revisions beyond the cap as free; they are a new request.

                              2. CC — Content Calendar & Brief Standard

                              The Content Calendar is the production brief. Each row must give the producer enough to start without follow-up questions. A title and a one-line idea are not enough. This section defines what the content or social team hands over, and when the producer may refuse a row.

                              One form, not two. For calendar work, a calendar row that passes Gate G0 (2.3) is the FR-01 brief. Nobody retypes it into a second form.
                              Where briefs come from. Calendar content: the calendar row (CC-01). Standalone creative work outside a calendar (an ad, a video, motion, animation, a photoshoot): brief it in JML Creative Brief Studio; its export is the FR-01. Website, UX research and UI work: JML Design Brief Studio; that work is outside this system.

                              2.3 Definition of Ready (Gate G0)

                              A row is Ready only when all of these are true:

                              • Every M field, and every C field for its type, is filled
                              • Copy is final and approved by the content owner (not "for review")
                              • Format comes from the fixed list and agrees with size and slide count (no "Text" plus "1 image" conflicts)
                              • Every asset has a source, an owner and a date
                              • People on screen have consent, or a plan to get it
                              • References are attached
                              • One final approver is named
                              • Lead time is enough. Suggested defaults: 3 working days for a static post, 5 for an edited video, 7 for motion graphics or AI video

                              If not ready: the producer returns the row within one working day with a list of what is missing. Production does not start. If the gap is not closed in time, the publish date moves (1.4).

                              2.5 Capture standard for client and team footage

                              Send this to anyone filming for JML. It decides how much editing time is lost later.

                              • Device: camera or recent phone, rear (main) camera
                              • Resolution: 4K if the device supports it, otherwise 1080p
                              • Frame rate: 30 fps (or 25), the same for the whole shoot; 60 fps only for slow motion
                              • Orientation: vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories, horizontal only if the brief says so
                              • Light: face a window or soft light; avoid strong backlight and mixed light colors
                              • Place and background: tidy, on-brand, no distracting signs or screens
                              • Framing: camera at eye level; chest-up for talking to camera; leave headroom; keep the face away from the top and bottom edges where app buttons sit
                              • Stability: tripod or gimbal
                              • Sound: wireless lavalier mic; quiet room without echo; no music playing in the background
                              • Test first: record 10 seconds and listen with headphones
                              • Clean the lens before filming
                              • Retakes: after a stumble, pause and repeat the whole sentence; with many pauses or mistakes, do the take again where possible
                              • B-roll: 3 to 5 seconds of steady footage for each point in the script
                              • Send originals through a Drive or WeTransfer link, or as a file. Never as a chat video in WhatsApp or Telegram, which compresses it

                              FileStandard
                              PhotosOriginal files, full resolution, no unnecessary compression or filters
                              VideoOriginal camera files, no re-export or quality loss
                              AudioWAV, or high-quality original, without noise

                              1. حاکمیت اصلی (Master Governance)

                              1.1 اصول بنیادین

                              1. بدون بریف، شروعی نیست. هیچ کاری بدون بریف تأییدشده آغاز نمی‌شود.

                              6. بدون تأیید نهایی چیزی منتشر نمی‌شود.
                              7. فایل‌های قابل‌ویرایش و خروجی‌های نهایی را جدا نگه دارید.

                              • افراد حاضر در تصویر رضایت‌نامه دارند یا برنامه‌ای برای گرفتن آن هست
                              • رفرنس‌ها پیوست شده
