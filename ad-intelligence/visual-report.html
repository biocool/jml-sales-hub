Paid vs public content

The difference is architectural, not just visual.

Creator-style appearance is no longer evidence that a post is organic. Distribution, disclosure, destination, authorization, and measurement provide the more reliable distinction.

TikTok warning: 24 of 100 public records carried a commercial-disclosure flag. Several records without that flag still used terms such as #ad or partner in the caption.

      Hook and CTA pattern library

      Attention patterns and action patterns are different systems.

      A hook earns the next second or the click. A CTA turns that attention into one next step. The clusters below separate visual and narrative hooks from commercial actions.

      How to use the examples: select any linked example to open its creative, classification, source metadata, and destination in one dashboard-style modal.

      Hook clusters

      Grouped by what interrupts attention or creates a reason to continue.

      Observed pattern: public YouTube leans harder on thumbnail curiosity, emotion, and genre recognition. Paid creative more often connects the opening hook to an offer, product, or destination.

      CTA clusters

      Grouped by the decision the ad or destination asks the person to make.

      Classification note: CTA labels use the ad title and destination when the provider record does not expose the exact button copy.

      Proposed tests

      What JML can test next.

      These are evidence-informed proposals. They are not approved strategy and should not be treated as performance guarantees.

      Build one message system, not one universal asset.

      Keep the commercial promise stable. Redesign the execution for each platform.

      Test the first three seconds.

      Lead with a face, result, demonstration, or explicit problem on short-form video.

      Create 6, 15, and 30 second video cuts.

      Add native 9:16 versions instead of relying only on 16:9 mobile inventory.

      Use modular creative families.

      Vary one headline, benefit, audience, proof point, or offer at a time.

      Match direct-response ads to dedicated pages.

      Repeat the promise, keep one decision primary, and minimize required fields.

      Scale proof to decision risk.

      Simple signup pages can stay short. Complex B2B decisions need detail, examples, and risk reduction.

      Version landing pages with campaigns.

      Prevent a changing homepage from weakening message continuity while an ad remains active.

      Separate measurement layers.

      Track attention, engagement, intent, conversion, and business outcomes independently.

      Methodology and limitations

      The paid dataset contains 100 records per platform or device view and is ranked by estimated impressions. It is not a random market sample. Spend, impressions, and CPM are provider estimates. The public-trend dataset is not an unpaid-only control group. TikTok includes disclosed and undisclosed commercial content. Pinterest provides limited post-level performance evidence. Missing metrics and inaccessible media were not estimated. Recommendations remain proposals until an explicit human decision creates a new approved strategy status or version.
