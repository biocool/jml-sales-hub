QUESTIONS

Everything to know
before you start.

Will this guarantee a higher Google ranking?+

No. Posting and maintenance support accurate information and customer engagement. Rankings, leads and revenue are not guaranteed.

Do you need my Google password?+

No. Use the appropriate business-profile manager invitation. Keep ownership with your business.

Can you help request reviews?+

Yes, within an approved scope and appropriate consent. No incentives, fabricated reviews or positive-only review requests.

How is the scope decided?+

We confirm locations, review volume, content needs, the approval process and reporting first. Website changes and historical review catch-up are separately scoped when needed.
