Flyer structure:
1. “Prepared for [BUSINESS]”
2. Benefit-led headline tied to the business goal
3. Up to 3 verified, contextualized observations
4. One clear next step
5. Unique QR to the matching business page
6. Source date and independent-JML disclosure

No data available? Say “Not measured.” Never turn missing data into zero or label it a failure.
Release checklist
  1. Recheck facts and dates immediately before use.
  2. Approve the destination, real contact route and any offer.
  3. Publish the intended audience version; replace private QR links.
  4. Scan both QR codes on a phone outside the owner’s session.
  5. Remove internal-review banners only after approval.
  6. Record permissions, source, channel, owner, next step and opt-outs.
