01 / Setup

02 / Shared foundation

Agree on the outcome and audience.

Desired change; primary KPI, proposed target and measurement window

Why will this matter to the audience now?
What is being promoted? Include the promise, evidence and restrictions.
Education, trust, community or promotion? Define the voice and how this fits the content plan.

03 / Shared foundation

Shape the message

Publish-ready caption and relevant hashtags; avoid unsupported claims
Funnel stage, conversion event, landing page URL and tracking plan.

Align the handoff

Creative brief vs. content brief

A creative brief defines the objective, audience, message and creative direction. A content brief adds the details of what to write or say: structure, sources, hook and CTA. This studio combines both, then adds the production module your deliverable needs.

The structure behind this studio

Keep the strategy shared across formats, then add the execution details. This is JML’s proposed workflow, informed by Milanote’s separate advertising, social, design and video template categories. It is not a Milanote product or copied template.

  • Advertising reference: a focused proposition and business objective.
  • Social reference: channel, voice and campaign context.
  • Design reference: scope, visual examples and what to avoid.
  • Video reference: important shots and production direction.
