01 / FIELD KEY

Eight fields, the same numbering in every style

The placement changes by preset. Page numbers and swipe cues are not brief fields.

1Topic label
Short subject or decision area.
2Series stamp
Two lines: series name and industry or case-study category.
3Industry icon
Small sector marker next to the stamp, not the main visual.
4Main headline
Largest cover text. Name a recognizable problem or tension.
5Context & takeaway
Briefly explain the setup and what the reader should notice.
6Metric card
One supported number or comparison with its unit. It can also be unboxed.
7Fixed brand footer
Use the approved footer unchanged in production; only a placeholder appears here.
8Hero illustration
The topic-specific artwork: paper layers, photo and sketch, or photo and contours.

PRESET 01

Layered paper animal

Choose this when an animal silhouette and an inset scene can communicate the decision.

View prompt
Create one isolated editorial paper-cut [species, optionally sex/age if relevant] for a personal LinkedIn editorial series. Its visual metaphor is [one concrete decision or contrast]. Use [approved same-species artwork or accurately identified anatomy reference] for natural proportions, pose and expression, and [approved treatment reference] for depth, palette and material only. Do not copy another species' anatomy.

Show [specific gesture/view], with believable [key species features]. Compose the animal as a dominant lower-right cover hero with the whole silhouette inside the frame and slightly more breathing room toward [direction]. Keep the head and tail recognizable at thumbnail size. Use layered berry/magenta, deep plum, warm mustard/gold and cream/kraft paper; let the exact approved reference override these defaults. Use grain on matte paper, visible cut faces and subtle, consistent directional contact shadows. Make successive cut edges visually distinct, not merely stripes over flat fur.

Cut a clearly recessed window wholly inside the [torso/abdomen/shell]. Inside it, use [one primary motif] and [up to two simple supporting forms] to communicate [the decision a viewer should be able to infer]. Keep the inset visually separate from fur or feather markings; use a clean focal silhouette and generous contrast. Do not imply a causal or sequential relationship the post doesn't establish.

Produce an animal-only cutout with true transparent surroundings, clean alpha edges and no cast shadow outside the silhouette. No lettering, numbers, charts, logos, portrait, full slide, scenery outside the animal, border, or watermark. Avoid gradients that mimic plastic, neon, oversized cartoon eyes and icon outlines. The render is a visual target only, not evidence of a verified layer count.

PRESET 02

Photographed object + hand-drawn sketch

Choose this when a real object can become a working part of the drawn metaphor.

View prompt
Create one isolated editorial illustration on true transparent alpha. The core idea is [decision, contrast or mechanism]. Use one realistically photographed [physical object with recognizable material] that literally becomes [sketched metaphor]. Make this join necessary to the meaning, not decoration: the object must physically connect to the drawing with matching scale and contact. Use loose ink and graphite, irregular handmade marks, subtle fiber or pencil texture, asymmetry, and one controlled accent from the brand palette. Preserve the supplied real object reference if used. No letters, chart elements, headshot, posts, footer, backdrop panel, cartoon gloss, stacked paper-cut layers or generic sector clip art.

PRESET 03

Stock photograph + flowing force lines

Choose this when a licensed real photograph explains the subject and the contour sweep reinforces the composition.

View prompt
Use a licensed real stock photograph of [topic-specific subject doing or showing something meaningful], not a generated stand-in. [Reason the subject explains the post.] On a deep plum ground, let the photo emerge organically on the right/lower-right. Sweep a single broad band of fine, continuous pale ivory-gray contours from upper right to lower left. Sculpt two or three softly flowing bends: let the contours fan out, compress at the turn, and open again with variation across the band. Disperse several thin mustard strands among the pale ones; never stack them into one yellow stripe. The current should respond to the subject and skirt its focal point. Do not add disconnected lines, nodes, HUDs, outlined blobs or a framed photo rectangle. Keep the upper left quiet and dark for the locked page typesetting. Don't render words, diagrams, numbers or logos as part of the art.

Before publishing

Keep the cover at 1080 × 1350 and check it at phone size. Put the evidence and source on slide two. Label illustrative numbers, verify the license of any photo, and get approval before posting.
