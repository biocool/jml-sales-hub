How these screening labels work

Buyer type reflects the organization’s legal or governance status, not its funding. Contract complexity reflects delivery and bidding obligations: Moderate = bounded scope, High = multiple workstreams or specialist requirements, Very high = major integrations, multi-phase delivery or specialist staffing.

Competition difficulty is a JML estimate of how hard it would be to stand out based on stated experience, references and specialist requirements. Unknown means the available evidence does not justify a single rating. This is not a bidder count or win probability. JML service match links to the current Services catalog and may cover only part of the scope.
